The Disaster Cycle

A recurring catastrophic cycle — proposed every ~6,000 to ~12,000 years — driven by solar activity, geomagnetic field collapse, and crustal instability. Summarizes major stages from independent researchers correlated with NOAA and NASA data.

Sources: Suspicious0bservers (Ben Davidson) · The Ethical Skeptic (ECDO) · Craig Stone / Nobulart · ECDO Monitor · Zacharias
1
Stage 1 — Where we've been
Solar Cycle Peak & Increasing Activity
The Sun operates on an ~11-year sunspot cycle. During solar maximum, sunspots surge with more intense flares and CMEs. SC25 has significantly exceeded forecasts. Researchers point to a larger ~6,000 and ~12,000-year grand cycle linked to geomagnetic excursion events in the paleomagnetic record.
SC25 50%+ above forecast~11yr / ~6Kyr / ~12Kyr cycles
NOAA SWPC — Solar Cycle Progression (live)
2
Stage 2 — Currently underway
Geomagnetic Field Weakening
Earth's magnetic dipole has been weakening at an accelerating rate — ~25–35% from its Holocene peak. The South Atlantic Anomaly continues expanding. A potential North Atlantic Anomaly has been identified. This makes Earth increasingly vulnerable: less shielding means even moderate solar events become dangerous.
~25–35% field lossSAA expanding
SpaceWeatherNews — Magnetospheric Risk by Kp & Field Loss
3
Stage 3 — Currently underway
Magnetic Pole Migration & Excursion
Magnetic north migrates at ~55 km/year (up from ~15 km/yr a century ago). Combined with field weakening, this signals a potential geomagnetic excursion. The Ethical Skeptic's ECDO theory proposes core-mantle decoupling via a Dzhanibekov effect mechanism.
~55 km/yr pole migration~12Kyr excursion cycle
Magnetic north pole — accelerating migration track
4
Stage 4 — Anticipated
Grid Vulnerability & Carrington-Class Events
As field weakens, grid-damage thresholds drop dramatically. A Carrington-class event would cause cascading transformer failures. The May 2024 G5 storm offered a preview. GICs would surge through power grids, pipelines, and undersea cables. Limited spare transformer inventory means recovery could take months to years.
1859 Carrington melted telegraph wiresLimited global spare transformers
NOAA Geospace — Real-time magnetosphere (live)
5
Stage 5 — Theoretical culmination
Solar Micronova & Magnetosphere Collapse
A solar micronova — an extreme outburst far exceeding normal flares. Other Sun-like stars produce such outbursts on cyclical timescales. Apollo lunar soil containing Al-26 suggests past events. Ice cores show isotope spikes at ~12,000-year intervals consistent with sudden solar energetic particle bursts.
Micronovae in Sun-like starsAl-26 lunar evidence
SOHO LASCO C3 — CME detection (live)
6
Stage 6 — Theoretical (ECDO)
Crustal Displacement & Pole Flip
The Ethical Skeptic's ECDO theory: core exothermic reactions + field collapse + solar input triggers an ~104-degree crustal displacement. Craig Stone's paleomagnetic analysis identifies supporting geological evidence. Earth oscillates between two stable rotational states, with transitions producing ocean displacement, volcanism, and climate reorganization.
~104° displacementDzhanibekov instability
Dzhanibekov effect — rotational instability
7
Stage 7 — Aftermath
Recovery, Re-stabilization & The Next Cycle
Earth settles into alternate rotational state. Transition involves massive ocean displacement, intense volcanic/seismic activity, prolonged atmospheric disruption. Eventually the field regenerates, Sun returns to baseline, and civilization rebuilds — until the cycle repeats. Understanding this cycle is the first step toward preparation.
Ocean displacementCycle resets — new equilibrium
